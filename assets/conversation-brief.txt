NORTHSTAR RETAIL / SYNTHETIC CONVERSATION BRIEF
This is a hypothetical practice case. Every person and event is fictional.
No real personnel record, diagnosis or disciplinary process is represented.

C01 | WORK CONTEXT | October 7, 2026
You lead a small operations enablement team. Morgan is a strong contributor
known for clear training materials and helpful peer support.
The purpose of this conversation is to understand recent delivery barriers
and agree realistic support and a checkpoint, not to decide discipline.

C02 | OBSERVABLE DELIVERY FACTS
The agreed first draft of a facilitator guide was due September 9, 2026.
It arrived September 12 without advance notice of a revised date.
A separate checklist update was due September 23 and arrived September 26.
Those dates were recorded in a shared project plan.
Do not generalize two missed dates into "always late."

C03 | DOCUMENTED WORK IMPACT
The first delay moved a peer review from September 10 to September 14.
The second left the coordinator one day rather than the planned four days
to check the update. No customer complaint, lost revenue or safety incident
is documented. Do not invent those impacts.

C04 | KNOWN UNKNOWNS
You have not yet asked Morgan what made the dates difficult.
Workload, prioritization, clarity and dependencies are questions, not facts.
Nothing is known about personal circumstances, health or motivation.
Morgan's likely reaction is unknown. Rehearsal reactions must be explicitly
hypothetical, not inferred personality traits or historical evidence.

C05 | SUPPORT AND BOUNDARIES
You can discuss reprioritizing work with the team, smaller interim milestones,
earlier risk flagging and clearer acceptance criteria.
You cannot promise additional budget or headcount.
Propose a mutually agreed checkpoint; no date is already accepted.
Use observable Situation-Behavior-Impact feedback, then ask an open question.
Listen before proposing a fix. Do not make employment or disciplinary
recommendations, infer protected characteristics, or write to an HR system.

Rehearsal controls:
The participant is the leader. The assistant plays Morgan.
One response at a time, then wait.
PAUSE = stop role-play and help rephrase.
DEBRIEF = end role-play and coach on the participant's actual words.
